## Why this works (one paragraph, delete before using)

Google ranks the SEC press release because .gov pages carry enormous authority. You don't beat that by clicking; you beat it by surrounding it. Each profile above does two things: it links to your site (which raises your whole domain's authority so your pages climb), and most of them rank on page one for "Edward M. Liceaga" themselves, so they physically occupy the slots the press release would otherwise hold. Ten claimed profiles plus your site plus LinkedIn plus two articles is enough to push a single .gov result from position one down toward the bottom of page one over a few months.
