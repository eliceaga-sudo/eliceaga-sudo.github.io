About

Edward M. Liceaga has spent more than 25 years on the revenue side of finance: originating, structuring, and closing across specialty finance, futures and options markets, and capital markets.

He began his career as an equity and commodity research analyst before moving into institutional sales of options-trading and risk-management software, then served as Managing Director of an investment banking capital markets group executing capital raises and M&A advisory mandates for middle-market clients, hedge funds, and private equity. He went on to found and co-found multiple investment firms focused on growth capital for small- and micro-cap companies, sourcing and closing deals across North America, Europe, Australia, and Asia-Pacific.

Today, he drives business development for a specialty finance firm offering working capital advances, lines of credit, asset-based loans, AR factoring, and bridge lending, and he leads sales for a trading automation platform serving active futures and options traders. He is an active NQ futures trader himself, which means he speaks the customer's language because he is one. He holds an MBA with a Derivatives concentration from Loyola University Chicago and writes about small-business financing and markets.
