## 1. CCAP (ccapsolution.com) — merchant funding leads

### What the research says
- The head term "merchant cash advance" is a trap: **low real search volume** (merchants search "business loan," not "MCA" — per deBanked's SEO analysis) and a locked SERP (NerdWallet, Bankrate, CNBC, big funders). The ~540K/mo demand pool lives in loans/working-capital/fast-funding variants.
- **Winnable SERPs found:** "same day business funding" (mid-size brokers rank), "MCA vs loan" comparisons (small broker sites like Lendzi/Swish rank today), "bank denied my business loan" (small funders rank), factor-rate/calculator queries (tiny sites rank), industry pages (salon funding SERP is nearly empty), Spanish-language SMB funding (only UCS, Greenbox, One Park compete).
- **The broker blueprint is United Capital Source:** 1,100+ Trustpilot reviews at 4.9★, BBB A+, state pages, Spanish hub, year-stamped titles. Reviews are the moat.
- **Paid is hostile:** Google restricts "cash advance" phrasing (payday-adjacent filters); loan CPCs run $10–50; Meta forces the financial-products special ad category (no granular targeting) and bans <90-day loan products. For a small broker, referral partnerships and leads beat head-term PPC. Lead-market comps: real-time web leads $20–60, live transfers $75–200.
- **Compliance:** FTC's $20.3M Braun judgment is the cautionary tale; CA SB 1235/SB 666, NY CFDL and ~9 state disclosure laws apply; Virginia requires MCA broker registration. Never "loan," never guaranteed amounts — which the mca skill already enforces.

### Top actions (in order)
1. **Build a factor-rate calculator page** (advance × factor rate → payback, est. APR, daily payment). Targets a winnable cluster, earns links, signals the transparency disclosure laws now reward.
2. **Own the "bank said no" cluster** — pillar ("Denied a business loan? Your real options") + spokes. Best intent-to-difficulty ratio found.
3. **Industry pages two-part format** (landing + guide): trucking (exists — expand), restaurants, salons (empty SERP), construction, HVAC.
4. **Attack "same day / fast funding," not "merchant cash advance."**
5. **Comparison set:** MCA vs business loan / line of credit / invoice factoring.
6. **Google Business Profile + review engine** (Google/Trustpilot/BBB): ask every funded merchant; add review schema.
7. **Spanish mini-hub** (3 pages): préstamos para negocios / mal crédito / capital de trabajo.
8. **HARO/expert-quote PR weekly as Edward** — the personal-brand domain makes this natural; DR70+ citations unlock YMYL rankings.
9. **Referral partnerships** (accountants, bookkeepers, POS/payment reps, equipment dealers) instead of buying head-term clicks.
10. **Repurpose each article into a 60-second Short/LinkedIn post.**

## 2. Kenetik (drinkkenetik.com, code EDDIEG) — DTC affiliate traffic

### What the research says
- **Kenetik's position:** "bioidentical ketone drink," anti-Ketone-IQ on taste, no "woozy" feel, and price; Informed Sport certified; Life Time alliance (Leadville, Grand Prix), athletes (Riley Amos, Cameron Jones), Roman Atwood as flagship creator; Kickbooster affiliate at 15% off / 15% commission.
- **The category leader's playbook (Ketone-IQ, ~$100M+):** podcasts (Huberman moment), celebrity equity (Bartlett, Jon Jones), Team Visma, retail (Target/Circle K), and the ketone.com content moat. You can't out-spend that — but the research shows **independent reviewers, not brands, win the "best/review/vs/does it work" SERPs**, and those winners are small blogs (Outliyr, Michael Kummer, Bodyketosis). That's the real estate an ambassador site can take.
- **Open gaps found:** "Kenetik vs Ketone-IQ" (only Kenetik's own page targets it), Informed Sport / drug-tested athlete queries (near zero competition; military/NCAA angle untouched), caffeine-free afternoon-crash cluster (Kenetik's cleanest differentiator), and "Kenetik promo code" — currently monetized by coupon aggregators instead of you.
- **Third-party ammo already exists:** Kummer's roundup names Kenetik best-tasting/best-value (~$3.54/serving concentrate); UBC clinical study; ProveIt confirms BHB elevation but flags cognition evidence as mixed — honest framing wins.
- **Compliance:** every EDDIEG post needs clear FTC disclosure (spoken + on-screen in video, above the fold in posts; penalties ~$53K/violation); structure/function claims only (focus, clarity, endurance, recovery) — never disease/weight-loss claims. The 2 PM Wall article's existing framing is on the right side of this.

### Top actions (in order)
1. **"Kenetik vs Ketone-IQ (2026): I tested both"** — highest-intent, lowest-competition keyword in the niche. Real photos, blood-ketone readings, taste/price tables. Repurpose to YouTube + Shorts.
2. **"Best ketone drinks 2026" roundup** with first-hand testing; corroborate the best-tasting/best-value superlatives reviewers already grant Kenetik.
3. **Own the Informed Sport / drug-tested niche:** "Ketone drinks safe for NCAA, military, first responders." (Builds directly on the existing Informed Sport article.)
4. **Evergreen Tour de France/endurance piece** refreshed each July + Leadville/Grand Prix content tagging Kenetik's sponsorships.
5. **Caffeine-free cluster:** "energy drink without caffeine," "3pm slump," "Celsius alternative without caffeine." (Extends The 2 PM Wall.)
6. **30-day self-experiment series** with BHB readings — skeptic-satisfying honesty is the moat, and it survives Google's review updates.
7. **Short-form video:** taste tests vs. the $5 shot, fasted-workout clips; product-first hooks.
8. **Disclosed, useful Reddit answers** (r/ketoscience, r/HubermanLab, r/cycling) linking your comparison post, not the store.
9. **Capture "Kenetik promo code" searches** with your own EDDIEG page; put the code + disclosure in every description, pinned comment, bio link.
10. **Compliance template:** standing disclosure block; claims checklist limited to structure/function language; ask Kenetik about running EDDIEG through Kickbooster so links and code both attribute.

## 3. Lexington Recovery (lexingtonrecovery.com) — B2B funder clients

### What the research says
- **The buyer universe is small and reachable:** MCA funders, syndicators/participation investors, fintech lenders, equipment lessors, debt buyers. Industry default rates run ~15–25%+; 2025-26 portfolio stress is the tailwind. Buyers discover collections partners mostly by **peer referral in forums** (DailyFunder literally has "which collections agency gave you the highest return?" threads), directories (Funder Intel's collection-company directory), and events — not Google.
- **Creditor-side SEO is thin:** merchant-defense law firms out-publish creditor-side firms 10–50x and own the head terms; only Mizrahi Law (attorney) and Dedicated GBC have real creditor-side clusters. Mizrahi even publishes "attorney vs agency — agencies lose" content that needs a rebuttal.
- **Nobody publishes numbers.** No competitor shows net-back %, time-to-first-dollar, or quantified case studies — and forum threads show buyers explicitly asking for exactly that. The existing /case-studies/ page is ahead of the field; quantifying it is the single biggest conversion lever.
- **Compliance-first is the winning story:** FDCPA is consumer-only, but CA SB 1286 (eff. July 2025) extends protections to commercial debts under $500K; FTC has banned MCA collectors (Richmond Capital) and won $20.3M (Braun) over abusive tactics; the Bloomberg COJ exposé still shapes the industry. Funders now fear their collector becoming their next headline — Lexington's stated "compliance-first, structured" posture is precisely what the moment buys.
- **The 2026 event circuit:** deBanked CONNECT Miami (Feb 12), Broker Fair NYC (June 1, The Glasshouse), Funders Forum + Broker Expo (FL Mar 4–6; NY Sep 15–16, sponsorships purchasable online). Dedicated and KBM reliably show up; rate cards via events@debanked.com.

### Top actions (in order)
1. **Get listed:** Funder Intel collection-company directory + partner placement; deBanked vendor ecosystem. Cheap and high-intent.
2. **Work the referral forums:** DailyFunder + deBanked Promotions board — intro post, then genuinely helpful answers. This is where competitors win deals for free.
3. **Quantify /case-studies/:** net-back % after fee, days-to-first-recovery, mechanism ("UCC 9-406 notices → funds restrained in 9 days → settled at 74¢"), role-attributed testimonials ("Head of Servicing, top-20 funder"). Nobody else publishes this.
4. **Publish the creditor-side content cluster** debtor-side firms can't credibly write: "The Funder's Default Playbook," UCC 9-406 notification guide, judgment domestication state-by-state, SB 1286 explainer, and the direct "agency vs attorney vs hybrid — what maximizes net-back" rebuttal. Two pieces/month, everything phrased "for funders."
5. **Trust credentials:** IACC membership + IACC/CLLA certification, licensing/bonding map, a Compliance & Governance page — the family-office/fintech diligence checklist.
6. **Join RBFC as a vendor member**; route outside counsel to the AFBA June conference.
7. **2026 events:** Miami (Feb), Broker Fair (June), Funders Forum (Mar/Sep). Request rate cards now.
8. **Founder-led LinkedIn:** 2–3 posts/week (SB 1286-type compliance changes, anonymized recovery breakdowns) + named-account outreach against the Funder Intel/deBanked funder directories. The buyer universe is a few thousand people; a named-account motion beats ads.
9. **"Portfolio recovery audit" wedge offer** for syndicators/family offices: lien position, UCC hygiene, expected net-back by vintage — converts audits into contingency placements.
10. **Trade-press bylines/podcasts** on "recovery without reputational risk," explicitly contrasting structured UCC enforcement with the FTC-sanctioned conduct.
